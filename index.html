Methods and limitations

Topics are estimated with TF-IDF and non-negative matrix factorization. Research lenses use keyword rules and may miss relevant posts or include incidental mentions.

VADER scores the tone of post text. Tone can miss irony, quotation, and game-specific slang, and cannot establish an author's beliefs, identity, or stance. The dashboard publishes aggregate counts and model terms.
